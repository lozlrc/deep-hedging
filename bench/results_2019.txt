Training: 30,000 steps per run, batch 256, lr 0.005, 3 seeds. Held-out: 500,000 paths, identical for every run.

| claim | paper | reproduced | mean trading gain |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Risk-neutral ATM price q | 1.69 | 1.6918 |  |

| Deep hedge price, 99% CVaR, no costs | 3.49 | 2.8567 ± 0.0459 | -0.0049 |
| 1% costs, 99% CVaR, recurrent network | 5.5137 | 4.6144 ± 0.0392 | -1.3775 |
| 1% costs, 99% CVaR, simpler network | 6.7446 | 4.6385 ± 0.0961 | -1.4212 |

Mean trading gain is E[P&L] + E[Z] on held-out paths: zero in an arbitrage-free market without costs, minus the average cost with them. A positive value would mean the policy is harvesting a flaw in the simulator rather than hedging.

Heston model hedge (stock + variance swap) on the same held-out paths: 50% CVaR 1.9462, 99% CVaR 3.4184, P&L mean -0.0093, std 0.3799.
